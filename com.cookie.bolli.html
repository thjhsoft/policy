Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2026 · App: BOLLI

This Privacy Policy explains how BOLLI ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency and compliance with applicable data protection laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

BOLLI does not require account registration and does not operate a personal user account server. Your game preferences (audio, language, visual quality, physics presets), custom arenas, custom color themes, and in-app coin balance are stored locally on your device. You can remove this data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the App.

2. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

To support the free availability of BOLLI, we use Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display ads (including App Open, Interstitial, and optional Rewarded ads).

  • Depending on your consent choices and region, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect or process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address / coarse location, device and OS information, crash/performance diagnostics, and ad interaction metrics to deliver, measure, and prevent fraud in advertising.
  • You can learn more about how Google uses data from apps that use its services at: Google Privacy & Terms.

3. GDPR / EEA & UK Consent Management

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App requests your consent via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) before initializing Google Mobile Ads or requesting personalized/non-personalized advertisements.

You may review, modify, or withdraw your consent preferences at any time from the Ad Privacy Options entry inside the App's Settings screen (where applicable).

4. Rewarded Ads & Virtual Coins

Rewarded video ads are completely optional and opt-in. Coins earned by voluntarily watching rewarded ads have no real-world monetary value and are stored solely on your device.

5. Children's Privacy

BOLLI is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can address it promptly.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or app features. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, requests, or privacy concerns regarding BOLLI, please contact us at:
Email: thjhsoft@gmail.com

个人隐私协议

生效日期：2026年11月17日 · 应用名称：BOLLI

本《个人隐私协议》（以下简称“本协议”）旨在向您说明 BOLLI（以下简称“本应用”或“我们”）在您使用本应用期间如何处理相关信息。我们高度重视您的隐私与数据安全，并严格遵守包括欧盟《通用数据保护条例》（GDPR）及英国 GDPR 在内的适用法律法规。

一、本地存储的信息

BOLLI 无需注册或登录账号，我们不设立收集个人身份信息的后台服务器。您的游戏偏好设置（音效、语言、画质、物理规则配置）、自定义地图、自定义配色主题以及应用内金币余额仅保存在您的设备本地。您可随时通过清除应用数据或卸载本应用来删除上述本地数据。

二、广告服务与第三方 SDK（Google AdMob）

为保障 BOLLI 的免费提供与持续更新，本应用集成了 Google AdMob（Google 移动广告 SDK）及 Google 用户消息平台（UMP），用于展示开屏广告、插屏广告以及由您主动选择观看的激励视频广告。

  • 根据您所在地区及您的同意选择，Google AdMob 及其广告合作伙伴可能会收集或处理设备广告标识符（如 Android Advertising ID）、IP 地址及大致地理位置、设备型号与系统版本、崩溃/性能诊断数据以及广告展示与互动数据，用于广告投放、效果衡量及反欺诈校验。
  • 关于 Google 如何处理来自合作应用的数据，请查阅：Google 隐私权和条款。

三、GDPR（欧盟 / 欧洲经济区及英国）合规与同意管理

针对位于欧洲经济区（EEA）、英国及瑞士的用户，本应用会在初始化 Google 移动广告 SDK 及请求任何广告前，通过 Google 官方 UMP 同意管理框架向您展示 GDPR 隐私同意消息，仅在获得合法同意后才会初始化广告服务。

适用地区的用户可随时在应用内的「系统设置」→「广告隐私设置」入口重新查看、修改或撤回您的广告同意偏好。

四、激励视频广告与金币说明

激励视频广告完全遵循用户自愿主动触发原则。您主动观看激励广告所获得的金币仅在设备本地记录与使用，不具有任何现实货币价值或现金兑换属性。
