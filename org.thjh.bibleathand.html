Privacy Policy (English)

Commitment to Pure Reading & Complete Privacy:
Thank you for choosing Bible At Hand (org.thjh.bibleathand). We hold deep respect for your spiritual devotion, meditation, and personal privacy. This application is designed as a pure, standalone, distraction-free Holy Bible reader. It does NOT contain any commercial advertisements, does NOT collect any personal identifiable information, and stores all your data strictly on your local device.

1. Information Collection and Storage

We believe scripture reading and spiritual prayer should be confidential, safe, and undisturbed:

  • No Account Required: You can use all features of the application without signing up, logging in, or providing any personal details such as email, phone number, name, or social accounts.
  • 100% Local Storage: All your personal bookmarks, reading footprints, and reader preference settings (font size, themes, line spacing) are stored exclusively in your device private sandboxed database (SQLite) and local preferences. They are never transmitted to external servers or third parties.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: Bible At Hand does not require or request sensitive permissions, such as Contacts, Camera, Microphone, Precise Location, or Phone Identifiers.

2. Zero Advertising & No Tracking SDKs

To preserve sacred reverence and peaceful devotion:

  • This application contains zero third-party advertisement SDKs (no Google AdMob, no Unity Ads, no Meta Audience Network, etc.).
  • We do not integrate behavioral tracking or user telemetry tools. We never monitor your reading habits, search queries, or devotional schedules.

3. Offline Availability & Text-To-Speech (TTS)

The scriptures (Chinese Union Version & King James Version) are bundled locally within the application package for instant offline reading:

  • The built-in audio narration operates using Android native on-device Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine. Voice synthesis occurs locally on your smartphone without uploading text or audio streams.

4. Scripture Art Card Export & Scoped Storage

When you choose to export and save a Scripture Art Card into your photo gallery, the application utilizes Android modern MediaStore API (Scoped Storage). Images are written directly to your system picture gallery without requesting broad read/write access to your personal files or media library.

5. Children Privacy

Bible At Hand is suitable for users of all ages, including families and children (PEGI 3 / Everyone). We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in applicable jurisdictions). The app remains completely offline and safe for everyone.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact:

Email: thjhsoft@gmail.com
