WordBloom Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Summary: WordBloom is designed with user privacy first. We do not require account registration, and your gameplay progress, learned vocabulary, bookmarks, and settings are stored locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

WordBloom does not collect or transmit personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number) to our private servers.

  • Local Game Data: Levels completed, stars, vocabulary bookmarks, mistake notebook, and sound preferences are stored securely on your device using local SQLite database storage.
  • Analytics & Diagnostics: Anonymous diagnostic information may be processed by Google Play services to ensure app stability and crash reporting.

2. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

WordBloom displays advertisements through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to support continuous free development and updates:

  • Google Mobile Ads: May collect anonymous identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID), device information, and IP address in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and applicable privacy regulations (such as GDPR and CCPA).
  • You can manage or reset your Advertising ID at any time in your Android device's Settings > Google > Ads.

3. Text-to-Speech & Audio

The English pronunciation feature uses the standard Android TextToSpeech engine integrated in your device operating system. WordBloom does not record or upload your voice or microphone data.

4. Children's Privacy

WordBloom is an educational and entertainment word game suitable for general audiences of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

5. Data Security & Offline Play

All core gameplay, puzzle generation, vocabulary learning, and worksheet printing functions are fully functional offline without requiring an active internet connection.
