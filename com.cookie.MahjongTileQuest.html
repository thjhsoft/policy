二、我们如何收集与使用信息

1. 本地存储的数据（非个人身份信息）

为了保障游戏的正常运行与断点续玩体验，本游戏会在您的设备本地（Internal Storage / SharedPreferences）保存以下数据：

  • 关卡进度与棋盘状态：当前正在进行的关卡编号、剩余牌位布局、剩余牌数、已消除对数、打乱洗牌与撤销历史。
  • 游戏战绩统计：已通关局数、当前连胜与历史最高连胜、各关卡最快通关时间与最高雅分。
  • 用户偏好设置：背景音乐音量、音效音量、静音状态、语言选择（简体中文/繁体中文/英文）、微暗显示被遮挡牌选项。

注意：上述数据纯本地保存，不会上传到任何私有服务器。您可随时在游戏首页点击“删除进度”或在 Android 系统设置中清除应用数据以彻底抹除这些记录。

2. 第三方广告服务收集的信息（Google AdMob）

为了维持本游戏的持续开发与免费运营，我们接入了由 Google LLC 提供的 Google AdMob 广告服务（包括开屏广告 App Open Ad 与关卡结算插屏广告 Interstitial Ad）。 Google AdMob SDK 可能会自动收集以下设备及广告交互数据：

  • 广告标识符（Google Advertising ID / AD_ID）：用于向您投放合规广告、频次控制、反广告欺诈以及衡量广告展示效果。
  • 设备软硬件信息：设备型号、操作系统版本、屏幕分辨率、系统语言、网络连接类型（Wi-Fi/移动数据网络）。
  • 大致位置数据：通过 IP 地址衍生的大致国家或地区（用于投放符合所在地区的广告）。
  • 广告交互数据：广告展示次数、点击记录、广告加载与关闭事件。

1. Introduction & Core Principles

Welcome to Mahjong Tile Quest (Package: com.cookie.mahjongtilequest), an offline-friendly, contemplative tile-matching puzzle game developed with care. We are firmly committed to safeguarding your privacy. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding data collection, usage, storage, and your rights when using our mobile application.

Key Summary: This game does not require account registration or login. We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, phone number, physical address, contacts, or financial details. Gameplay progression and audio preferences remain stored locally on your device.

2. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

A. Local Device Storage (Non-PII)

To provide seamless gameplay and state persistence, the app saves the following records locally on your device using Android internal storage:

  • Game State & Layout: Current level, remaining tile positions, removed pair count, undo history, and shuffle counts.
  • Statistics & Achievements: Completed games count, current streak, best streak record, fastest completion times, and highest scores.
  • User Preferences: Background music volume, sound effects volume, mute toggles, language choice (Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English), and tile shading toggle.

Note: This data is stored strictly on your local device and is never uploaded to any remote proprietary database. You can clear this data at any time via "Delete Progress" on the home screen or via Android Application Settings.

B. Third-Party Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

To keep this game free and support ongoing updates, we utilize Google AdMob for serving App Open Ads and Interstitial Ads (at stage completion intervals with frequency capping). The Google AdMob SDK may automatically collect certain device and diagnostic data:

  • Advertising Identifier (Google AD_ID): Used for frequency capping, ad fraud prevention, and performance attribution.
  • Device & Network Information: Device model, OS version, screen size, language, and connection type (Wi-Fi or cellular).
  • Coarse Location: Coarse geographic region derived from IP address for delivering regionally relevant ads.
  • Ad Event Telemetry: Impressions, click events, and ad load/dismiss performance metrics.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We integrate the following certified third-party service provider:

  • Provider: Google LLC (Google Play Services & Google AdMob)
  • Purpose: Ad delivery, ad impression verification, and cheat prevention.
  • Data Collected: Advertising ID (AD_ID), device metadata, IP-derived approximate location, and ad performance telemetry.
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Ad Settings & Personalization Controls: https://adssettings.google.com

4. Your Advertising ID (AD_ID) Controls & Opt-Out

You can manage or opt out of personalized advertisements on your Android device at any time:

  • Open your device's Settings.
  • Navigate to Google ➔ Ads.
  • Select "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID".
  • Once disabled or deleted, ad networks cannot link ad requests to your prior profile, and you will receive contextual ads only.
