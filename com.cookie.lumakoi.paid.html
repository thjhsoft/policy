LumaKoi Pro Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026 · Package: com.cookie.lumakoi.paid (Ad-Free Paid Edition)

Zero Ads · Zero Ad SDKs

1. Overview

LumaKoi Pro is designed as a pure, offline-first, ad-free personal courtyard koi pond experience. We respect your privacy and do not collect, sell, or share personal information.

2. No Advertising & No Tracking SDKs

This paid edition contains no advertisements, no third-party advertising SDKs, and does not access your device Advertising ID. No behavioral analytics or cross-app tracking is performed.

3. Local Game Data

Your pond state (coins, koi collection, growth progress, fish names, courtyard themes, audio/visual preferences, and daily reward records) is stored exclusively on your device. No account, login, or cloud profile is required.

4. Optional Local Weather & Region Sync

Only when you explicitly enable Sync Local 24h Weather in Settings does the app perform coarse IP-based city lookup or query public Open-Meteo geocoding and weather APIs for your selected city. GPS hardware location permissions are never requested, and weather queries are never linked to any personal identity.

5. Local Custom Audio Access

Audio files placed in the app's bundled assets or external app-specific music folder are read locally for playback only and are never uploaded or transmitted.

6. Children's Privacy

Because LumaKoi Pro does not collect personal information or serve third-party ads, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children or adults.

7. Data Deletion & Contact Us

All locally saved courtyard progress can be removed at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling LumaKoi Pro.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: thjhsoft@gmail.com

LumaKoi Pro 隐私协议

更新日期：2026 年 · 应用包名：com.cookie.lumakoi.paid（无广告付费版）

零广告 · 无广告 SDK

1. 概述

LumaKoi Pro 致力于为您提供纯净、离线优先、完全无广告的个人庭院锦鲤观赏体验。我们尊重您的隐私，不会收集、出售或共享您的个人信息。

2. 无广告与无追踪声明

本付费版本不包含任何广告，未集成任何第三方广告 SDK，也不会读取或使用设备广告 ID（Advertising ID），不进行跨应用追踪或行为画像。

3. 本地游戏数据存储

您的金币数量、锦鲤图鉴、成长进度、鱼儿名称、庭院主题布置、音画偏好以及每日签到记录均仅保存在您的设备本地，无需注册账号或登录。

4. 可选当地天气与昼夜同步

仅当您在设置中主动开启“同步当地天气与24小时时间”后，应用才会按需通过网络获取大致所在城市或您手动搜索城市的公开 Open-Meteo 气象数据。本应用绝不申请或读取 GPS 精确定位权限，也不会将地区信息与任何个人身份关联。

5. 本地自定义音频读取

放置在应用内置资源或应用专属音乐目录中的音频文件仅用于本地播放，绝不会上传至任何服务器。

6. 儿童隐私保护

由于 LumaKoi Pro 不收集个人身份信息且不含第三方广告，我们不会收集任何儿童或成人的个人数据。

7. 数据清除与联系我们

您可随时通过清除应用存储空间或卸载本应用，彻底删除所有本地庭院记录。

如您对本隐私协议有任何疑问，请通过电子邮件联系我们：thjhsoft@gmail.com
