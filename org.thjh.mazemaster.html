Privacy Policy

Application Name: Maze Master (Package ID: org.thjh.mazemaster)
Developer: thjhsoft
Effective Date / Last Updated: November 20, 2026

Thank you for choosing Maze Master (hereinafter referred to as the "App"), developed and operated by thjhsoft ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how data is handled when you use our App. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using the App.

1. Information Collection and Use

1.1 Personal Information & Email Address (Voluntary Support Inquiries Only)

The App does not require user registration or account login. By default, the App does not actively collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, phone number, or physical address on or off your device during normal gameplay.

However, if you voluntarily choose to contact us via email for customer support, bug reports, or feedback (Developer Communications), we will receive your Email Address and any information you choose to include in your message. This information is used strictly to respond to your inquiries and improve the App functionality, and we do not share or sell your email address to any third parties.

1.2 Third-Party Advertising & SDK Data Collection (Google AdMob)

To support the free provision of the App, we integrate Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements. Using the INTERNET and com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permissions, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and transmit certain device and usage data off your device for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention purposes:

  • Device or Other IDs: Google Advertising ID (AAID), app set ID, and device identifiers used to serve ads, measure ad performance, and report advertising revenue.
  • Approximate Location: IP-based coarse location information used by AdMob for regional ad delivery and compliance.
  • App Interactions & Diagnostics: Ad views, clicks, app launch events, crash logs, and performance diagnostics related to ad rendering.

All data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. For detailed information on how Google collects and processes data, please review Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

1.3 Local Game Progress, Blueprints, and Image Sharing

  • Local Game Records & Maze Blueprints: Your maze solving times, progress records, and custom maze blueprints are stored locally on your device only. This data is never uploaded to our servers.
  • Print & Share Function: When you use the maze image export, print, or share feature, the generated maze image is temporarily processed in your device's local cache or media storage and shared via the Android system share sheet at your explicit request. We do not collect or upload your files.

2. Summary of Data Safety & Handling

Data Type Collection Method Purpose Required / Optional
Device or Other IDs (Advertising ID) Collected & Shared via Google AdMob SDK Advertising, Analytics, Fraud Prevention Automatic (Users can reset/opt-out in Android Settings)
Approximate Location, App Interactions, Diagnostics Collected & Shared via Google AdMob SDK Advertising, Analytics, App Stability Automatic
Email Address Provided voluntarily when contacting support Developer Communications, App Functionality Optional (Users can choose)

3. Data Retention, Deletion, and User Controls

  • Local Data Deletion: You can delete all locally saved game records and maze blueprints at any time by clearing the App's storage data in your Android system settings or by uninstalling the App.
  • Advertising ID Controls: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising at any time on your Android device by navigating to Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Email Deletion Request: If you have contacted us via email and wish to have your email correspondence and email address permanently deleted from our records, please send a deletion request to thjhsoft@gmail.com. We will process and complete your request within 30 days.

4. Protection of Children's Privacy

We attach great importance to the protection of children's privacy. We do not knowingly collect personal identification information from children. The App does not serve personalized behavioral advertisements to users identified as children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information (such as an email address) to us, please contact us at thjhsoft@gmail.com, and we will promptly delete such information.

5. Data Security

We and our third-party service providers implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures, including HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, to safeguard information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or data deletion requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of the App, please contact us at:

  • Developer: thjhsoft
  • Email: thjhsoft@gmail.com
