Privacy Policy - KJV Bible Pro (Ad-Free Edition)

Effective Date: September 2026  |  Developer: thjhsoft
100% Ad-Free, Pure & Private Devotion:
The KJV Bible Pro Edition is created specifically for believers seeking a quiet, sacred, and distraction-free Bible reading experience. This paid edition contains zero advertisements, zero ad tracking libraries, and does not request the Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) permission. All your notes, prayers, highlights, and bookmarks remain entirely offline on your personal device.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

We respect your spiritual privacy and do not collect any personal data:

  • No Accounts or Registration: You never need to sign in, provide an email address, name, or phone number.
  • Zero Advertising SDKs: Unlike free ad-supported applications, this Pro version completely omits advertising SDKs and tracking frameworks.
  • No Analytics Tracking: We do not track your reading habits, session duration, or individual scripture searches.
  • No Advertising ID: The com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission is explicitly removed from the application manifest.

2. Local Storage and Data Ownership

You have full and exclusive ownership of all your reading data:

  • Your reading progress, book selections, bookmarks, color highlights, custom notes, and personal prayers are stored exclusively within your device's private app sandbox.
  • Your notes and prayers are never uploaded to any cloud server or shared with any third party.
  • You can easily clear all your local data at any time via Settings > Clear All Data.

3. In-App Purchases and Billing

KJV Bible Pro is purchased directly through the Google Play Store as a one-time paid app. All payment processing and billing transactions are handled directly and securely by Google Play Billing. The developer never receives, stores, or processes any credit card or financial information.

4. 100% Offline Capability

The entire King James Version Bible (Old and New Testaments) is pre-packaged within the app. You can study, search, bookmark, and reflect on scripture anywhere in the world without requiring an internet connection.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is completely safe for users of all ages, including children under 13. We do not collect any personal information whatsoever.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy if necessary to comply with relevant legal or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted here with an updated effective date.
