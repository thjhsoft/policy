Privacy Policy

App: Hanoi Tower (org.thjh.hanoitower) · Last updated: November 24, 2026

Thank you for playing Hanoi Tower. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect and Local Storage

Hanoi Tower does not require user registration or account login, and the developer does not directly collect, store, or sell your personal information on our own servers.

To provide a smooth gameplay experience, the following preferences and progress data are stored locally on your device:

  • Best move counts, completion times, star ratings, and unlocked achievements across game modes.
  • App settings such as selected theme, ring skin, background music, and sound effects preferences.

You can clear this local data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the application.

2. Third-Party Advertising & Consent

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements (such as App Open Ads and Interstitial Ads) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage user privacy consent where required.

When serving and measuring ads, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process certain device and usage information, including:

  • Device Advertising ID (or similar resettable identifiers)
  • IP address (used for general region estimation and fraud prevention)
  • Device model, operating system version, language, and app diagnostics/crash data
  • Ad interaction events (such as ad impressions and clicks)

For more information on how Google uses data from apps that use its services, please review Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information through third-party services, please contact us so we can address the issue promptly.

4. Data Security

Your game progress and settings remain on your device. Any network communication with advertising SDKs is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app or applicable legal requirements. Updates will be reflected in the latest version of the app and on our online policy page.
