ReadSpace (Ad-Supported Free Edition)

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 6, 2026
Application: ReadSpace (Package: com.cookie.readspace)
Developer: thjhsoft

Thank you for using ReadSpace ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are firmly committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and how your privacy is safeguarded.

1. Local Storage & Document Processing (100% Offline)

ReadSpace is an offline document and e-book reader designed to open and render formats including EPUB, PDF, TXT, MOBI, AZW3, FB2, CBZ, and CBR.

  • Document Access: The App requires permission to access your device storage solely to scan, import, and display e-books and documents chosen by you.
  • No Cloud Upload: Your documents, notes, bookmarks, reading progress, and custom settings remain 100% on your local device. We do not operate remote servers that collect, copy, upload, or sync your e-books or personal notes.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

The free edition of ReadSpace is supported by advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK: The App incorporates Google Play Services Ads SDK. During ad delivery, Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers such as the Google Advertising ID (AD_ID), IP address, general coarse device location, and anonymous app interaction metrics to deliver relevant, compliant ads and prevent fraudulent impressions.
  • Privacy Standards: AdMob processes information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and Google Play Developer Program Policies.
  • Opting Out: You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset/Delete Advertising ID.

3. System Permissions

  • Storage / Media Permissions: Required to locate, parse, and render user-selected books and documents.
  • Notification Permission: Optional. Used solely to display playback controls in the notification shade and lock screen when using the Text-to-Speech (TTS) audiobook feature.
  • Internet & Network State: Used solely by Google AdMob to load advertisements. The core e-book reading features operate completely without an internet connection.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

ReadSpace is a general audience application and does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will promptly take appropriate action.

5. Data Retention & User Control

All reading records, notes, and preferences can be reset or cleared at any time by clearing the App's data in system settings or uninstalling the application.

6. Contact Us
