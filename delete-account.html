Account & Data Deletion Request

This page provides instructions on how to request the deletion of your account and associated game data for Merge Tower Defense, published by THJHSoftware.

1. How to Delete Leaderboard Data & Disconnect Account

Merge Tower Defense uses Google Play Games Services for authentication and leaderboard scores. You can remove your game progress and revoke account access anytime directly on your Android device:

  1. Open the Settings app on your Android device.
  2. Tap Google ➔ All services (or Settings for Google apps).
  3. Select Play Games.
  4. Tap Delete Play Games account & data.
  5. Under "Delete individual game data", find Merge Tower Defense and tap Delete.

2. Data Types Deleted or Retained

  • Deleted: Leaderboard records, high scores, achievements, and Google Account authentication links.
  • Retained: Local game save data stored on your device storage (can be cleared anytime via Android Settings ➔ Apps ➔ Merge Tower Defense ➔ Storage ➔ Clear Data).
