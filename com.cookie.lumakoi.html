LumaKoi Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026 · Package: com.cookie.lumakoi (Free Ad-Supported Edition)

GDPR & UMP Compliant

1. Overview

LumaKoi is designed as a calm, personal courtyard koi pond experience. We respect your privacy and minimize data collection. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled in the free ad-supported version of LumaKoi.

2. Local Game Data

Your pond state (coins, koi collection, growth progress, fish names, courtyard themes, audio/visual preferences, and daily reward records) is stored locally on your device. LumaKoi does not require an account, sign-in, or personal profile, and we do not operate user-tracking servers.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob) & GDPR Consent

To keep this edition free, LumaKoi integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to serve App Open Ads (which you can skip during the welcome animation) and opt-in Rewarded Ads (which you explicitly choose to watch for in-game coins).

  • Delayed Initialization & Consent (EEA / UK / Switzerland): For users in the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland, AdMob initialization and ad requests are deferred until consent status is gathered through Google's official UMP GDPR consent message.
  • Data Processed by Google AdMob: Depending on your consent choices and region, Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), coarse IP-based location, app interaction events, diagnostics, and ad performance data to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads and prevent fraud.
  • Managing Your Ad Privacy Choices: You can update or withdraw your consent at any time via Settings → Ad Privacy Options (when applicable in your region), or reset/limit your device Advertising ID in Android system settings.

Learn more about how Google uses data in advertising: Google Privacy & Terms and Google Privacy Policy.

If you prefer a completely ad-free experience with zero advertising SDKs, you may optionally choose the standalone LumaKoi Pro paid edition on Google Play.

4. Optional Local Weather & Region Sync

Only when you explicitly enable Sync Local 24h Weather in Settings does the app perform coarse IP-based city lookup or query public Open-Meteo geocoding and weather APIs for your selected city. GPS hardware location permissions are never requested, and weather queries are never linked to a personal account.

5. Local Custom Audio Access

Audio files placed in the app's bundled assets or external app-specific music folder are read locally for playback only and are never uploaded or shared.

6. Children's Privacy

LumaKoi is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data Deletion & Contact Us

All locally saved courtyard progress can be removed at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling LumaKoi.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your privacy rights, please contact us at: thjhsoft@gmail.com
