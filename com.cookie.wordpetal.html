Privacy Policy

Welcome to WordPetal – Cozy English Word Game ("WordPetal", "we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to respecting and protecting the privacy of all players. This Privacy Policy details how information is handled when you download, install, and play WordPetal.

1. Information We Collect

WordPetal is built with a privacy-first, offline-capable architecture:

  • No Account Registration: We do not require you to create an account, log in with social media, or provide personal details such as your name, phone number, or physical address.
  • Local Gameplay Data: Your level progression, star ratings, earned coins, learned words, daily challenge streaks, and sound settings are saved strictly on your local device.
  • Advertising Identifiers (Optional / Google AdMob): When your device is connected to the internet, third-party advertising SDKs (specifically Google AdMob) may collect standard non-personal device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID), general device technical information, and coarse network location to serve contextual or interest-based ads in compliance with Google Play Developer policies.

2. How We Use Information

  • To persist your puzzle progress, unlocked botanical chapters, and vocabulary records locally on your device.
  • To deliver optional rewarded video hints (opt-in for free bonus coins) and non-intrusive transition ad breaks provided by Google AdMob.
  • To ensure app stability, smooth rendering performance, and responsive layout scaling across different Android phone and tablet screens.

3. In-App Purchases & Financial Information

WordPetal contains no in-app purchases (IAP) or paid subscription services. We do not collect, process, or store credit card, debit card, bank, or billing information. All levels, vocabulary definitions, and puzzle mechanics are entirely accessible through gameplay.

4. Data Storage & User Control

All game progress is maintained directly in your device's private application storage. You retain complete control over your data:

  • Reset Progress: You can wipe all game progress, stars, coins, and learned word records anytime through the in-game Settings → Reset Progress option.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling WordPetal deletes all associated local application data from your device.
  • Ad Personalization Opt-Out: You can opt out of personalized advertisements or reset your Android Advertising ID at any time in your device settings via Settings → Google → Ads.

5. Children's Privacy

WordPetal is designed to be family-friendly and suitable for players of all ages. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and Google Play Families policy. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that any personal information has been inadvertently gathered, please contact us immediately so we can promptly address it.

6. Third-Party Services

WordPetal utilizes trusted third-party mobile services governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Core application platform runtime. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob: Mobile advertising network delivering app-open, interstitial, and opt-in rewarded ads. (Google Ad Policies)

7. Changes to This Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect improvements in our game or updates to regulatory requirements. The latest version will always be accessible within the application and provided on our Google Play Store listing.
