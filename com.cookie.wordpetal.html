Privacy Policy

Welcome to WordPetal – Cozy English Word Game ("WordPetal", "we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data while providing a calm, educational, and enjoyable offline-ready word puzzle experience.

1. Information We Collect

WordPetal is designed with a Privacy-First & Offline-Capable architecture:

  • Local Gameplay Progress: Your level progress, star ratings, coin balance, learned vocabulary, daily challenge streaks, unlocked storybook chapters, and audio preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Advertising & Analytics Identifiers (Optional / AdMob): When connected to the internet, third-party ad networks such as Google AdMob may collect non-personal device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), coarse location, and crash diagnostics to display contextual or personalized advertisements in compliance with Google Play policies.
  • In-App Purchases: All billing transactions are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We never collect, process, or store your credit card or banking details.

2. How We Use Information

  • To save and restore your puzzle progress, learned words, and storybook collection locally on your device.
  • To provide optional rewarded hints, daily bonus coins, and interstitial ad breaks supported by Google AdMob.
  • To improve game stability, puzzle difficulty balance, and compatibility across Android screen sizes.

3. Data Storage & User Control

All gameplay data is stored locally in your device's private application storage. You can reset or erase your saved data at any time through the in-game Settings → Reset Progress option or via your Android system settings.

You may also opt out of personalized advertising at any time on your Android device by navigating to Settings → Google → Ads and selecting "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID".

4. Children's Privacy

WordPetal is a family-friendly educational word game suitable for learners of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information such as names, physical addresses, or phone numbers from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe any personal information was inadvertently submitted to us, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

5. Third-Party Services

Our app may integrate standard Google Play services subject to their own privacy terms:

  • Google Play Services & Google Play In-App Billing
  • Google AdMob (Mobile Advertising)
