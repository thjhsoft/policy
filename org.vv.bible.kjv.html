1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how thjhsoftware ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the KJV Bible mobile application (package name: org.vv.bible.kjv, the "App"). This is the free, ad-supported version of the App using Google AdMob. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about data practices.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect & Local Storage

No account registration or login is required. We do not operate our own backend servers and do not directly collect, upload, or sell personally identifiable information such as your name, phone number, or email address.

All user-created data is stored locally on your device, including:

  • Bookmarks, verse highlights, personal notes, and prayer entries;
  • Reading progress, reading plans, and search history;
  • App preferences such as font size, dark mode theme, and daily verse reminder settings.

If Android Auto Backup is enabled in your device settings, Android may back up your local app data to your personal Google account. You can delete all locally stored data at any time via Settings → Clear All Data inside the App, or by uninstalling the App.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob & UMP Consent)

To keep the App free, we integrate Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display banner ads and app open ads, together with the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to request and manage user privacy consent in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, Switzerland, and other applicable regions.

To serve, personalize (where consented), and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process:

  • Device and Advertising Identifiers: such as Google Advertising ID (AAID), device model, operating system version, language settings, and screen dimensions;
  • Network and Approximate Location Data: such as IP address (which may be used to estimate general location such as country or city);
  • Ad Interaction and Diagnostic Data: such as ad impressions, clicks, app performance, and crash diagnostics for ad delivery, frequency capping, attribution, and fraud prevention.

For more information on how Google collects and uses data, please review Google's official policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Ads Settings

The App may also use Google Play In-App Review to allow optional ratings, which is governed by Google Play's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. App Permissions

  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Required to load Google AdMob ads, retrieve privacy consent configurations, and open external links such as this Privacy Policy or Google Play when tapped.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, optional): Used on Android 13+ only if you enable the Daily Verse reminder feature, which schedules local notifications on your device. You may disable this anytime in the App's Settings or your device's system settings.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for ad serving and measurement.

When you use the verse card sharing feature, the App temporarily generates an image locally on your device solely so you can share it through the Android system share sheet.

5. Your Privacy Rights & Managing Ad Preferences

Depending on your location (including under the EU/UK GDPR and California CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access or delete your data, withdraw consent, and opt out of personalized advertising:

  • In-App Privacy Settings (EEA/UK and applicable regions): You can review, update, or withdraw your ad consent choices at any time via Settings → Privacy Settings inside the App.
  • Device Ad Controls: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads via your Android device's Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Local Data Deletion: Use Settings → Clear All Data inside the App or uninstall the App to permanently remove all locally stored data.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction, such as 16 in certain EEA countries). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data through the App or third-party ad services, please contact us using the email below and we will promptly assist.

7. Data Security

Your bookmarks, notes, prayers, and highlights remain in Android's sandboxed private app storage on your device, and network communications by third-party advertising SDKs are encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is 100% secure.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in app functionality, third-party SDKs, or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
