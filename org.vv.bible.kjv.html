Privacy Policy - KJV Bible

Effective Date: September 2026  |  Developer: thjhsoft
Our Commitment to Your Privacy:
Thank you for using the KJV Bible app. We hold deep respect for your spiritual devotion and privacy. This application does not require any account registration, does not collect personal identifiers, and stores all your personal reading notes, highlights, and bookmarks strictly on your local device.

1. Information Collection and Storage

We believe sacred reading should be peaceful, safe, and confidential:

  • No Account Required: You can access all scriptures and features without creating an account or providing email addresses, names, or phone numbers.
  • Local User Data: All your bookmarks, reading progress, notes, prayers, and highlights are stored locally on your device's private sandbox storage. They are never transmitted to any remote servers.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: We do not request sensitive device permissions such as Contacts, Camera, Microphone, Precise Location, or Phone Status.

2. Advertising and Analytics (Google AdMob)

This free edition is supported by advertising to maintain development and distribution costs:

  • We integrate Google AdMob to display banner ads and app open ads.
  • Google AdMob may collect pseudonymous identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / GAID), coarse IP location, and app diagnostics solely for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and frequency capping according to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Users can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via Android system settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
  • For a 100% ad-free experience with zero ad SDKs, users may choose the KJV Bible Pro edition.

3. Offline Availability

All 66 books of the Old and New Testament (King James Version) are bundled locally within the app package. The core reading experience is fully accessible offline without internet connectivity.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
