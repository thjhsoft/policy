PocketKit Compliance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Offline-First Privacy Architecture

PocketKit is engineered with strict offline-first principles. Your physical sensor data, measurements, calculations, and hardware readings are processed 100% locally on your device. We do not operate remote tracking servers, analytics trackers, or user profiling pipelines.

Zero Remote Transmission: Camera frames, sound samples, and step history never leave your smartphone.

2. Detailed Explanation of Special Permissions

PocketKit requests sensitive Android runtime permissions strictly to drive hardware sensor instruments. Here is how each permission is utilized:

  • Camera CAMERA
    Required exclusively for visual measurement tools: AR 3D Measure, Optical Color Detector, Cosmetic Mirror, and Digital Magnifier. Camera frames are processed in volatile memory in real time; no photos or videos are saved or transmitted.
  • Microphone / Audio RECORD_AUDIO
    Used solely by the Sound Meter tool to calculate ambient sound pressure levels (decibels dB) using mathematical FFT. Raw audio is never stored or recorded.
  • Geographic Location ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
    Required for GPS Locator, Altimeter calibration, Sun & Moon position ephemeris, Weather forecasts, and Star Finder astronomical math. Coordinates remain local to your device and are never tracked.
  • Physical Activity ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION
    Required on Android 10+ for the Pedometer tool to connect to the hardware step sensor. Step counts and calories are stored solely in your local device database.
  • Flashlight FLASHLIGHT
    Controls the hardware LED torch for the Flashlight utility and Morse code optical SOS signal generator.
  • Vibration & Haptics VIBRATE
    Provides tactile physical feedback for instrument dials, level balance zero-alignment, and button presses.
  • Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS
    Used on Android 13+ to display optional stopwatch/timer alerts and background pedometer daily goal notifications.
  • Network & Advertising ID INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, AD_ID
    Used for fetching live weather forecast data and serving compliant advertisements through Google AdMob in accordance with user consent preferences.

3. Advertising & CMP Consent (GDPR / CCPA)

We integrate Google AdMob to support free access to PocketKit. To protect European Economic Area (EEA) and UK users, we strictly implement the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) Consent Management Platform (CMP).

• User Choice: Ad components remain uninitialized until user consent choices are registered.

• Consent Revocation: You can modify or withdraw your advertising consent choices at any time from Settings > Ad Privacy Preferences.

• Screenshot Mode: Screenshots and ad-free flavors completely disable all ad SDK requests.

4. Security & Contact

We implement standard Android security sandboxing. If you have questions regarding our privacy practices, contact us via our developer portal.

1. 离线优先隐私架构

PocketKit 采用严格的“离线优先”原则构建。您的所有物理传感器数据、度量结果、计算公式以及硬件读数均 100% 在本地设备上实时计算。我们不设立远程追踪服务器，不收集任何用户行为画像或分析日志。

零远程传输： 相机画面、声音分贝样本、计步记录均仅保留于您的物理设备中。

2. 特殊系统权限详细说明

PocketKit 申请的敏感权限仅用于驱动物理传感器和测量工具，绝不滥用：

  • 相机权限 CAMERA
    仅用于视觉测量与光学工具：AR 三维测距、光学颜色检测器、前置镜子以及数字放大镜。图像流实时驻留于易失性内存中处理，绝不保存或上传任何照片或视频。
  • 麦克风录音权限 RECORD_AUDIO
    仅供“分贝噪音计”实时计算环境声压级（dB分贝）。音频数据仅在内存中做瞬时傅里叶变换计算，绝不录制、存储或回传任何音频片段。
  • 精确与粗略地理位置 ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
    用于 GPS 经纬度定位仪、气压海拔基准校准、太阳与月球运行星历、本地天气预报以及星空罗盘天体计算。位置信息完全保存在本地，绝无追踪。
  • 身体运动与步数识别 ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION
    用于 Android 10+ 设备的计步器工具，直接调用硬件步数检测器。历史步数仅保存在设备私有数据库中。
  • 闪光灯控制 FLASHLIGHT
    控制后置 LED 手电筒常亮与莫尔斯电码光电 SOS 应急求救信号。
  • 震动反馈 VIBRATE
    提供工业级触觉触感反馈，例如水平仪平衡归零、刻度微调及按键反馈。
  • 通知权限 POST_NOTIFICATIONS
    用于 Android 13+ 弹出秒表计时器结束提醒及后台计步目标达成提示。
  • 网络与广告标识符 INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, AD_ID
    用于在线天气预报更新，以及通过 Google AdMob 合规展示广告。广告请求完全遵照用户 CMP 授权设置。

3. 广告合规与 CMP 同意管理 (GDPR / CCPA)

为支持应用持续免费维护，本应用集成了 Google AdMob 广告服务。针对欧洲经济区 (EEA) 和英国用户，我们完整集成了 Google UMP (User Messaging Platform) CMP 授权机制：

• 合规先行： 未获得用户明确授权前，绝不预先初始化广告组件。

• 随时撤销： 您可随时在【设置】>【广告隐私偏好设置】中修改或撤回个性化广告授权。

• 截图模式： 截图专属渠道完全禁用广告组件，不发起任何广告网络请求。
