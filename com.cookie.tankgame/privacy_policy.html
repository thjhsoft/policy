1. Overview & Scope

Welcome to Cube Tank: 2D Tank Battle ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by THJH Soft. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy rights and ensuring the security of your personal data.

This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect when you install, play, or interact with our game, how that information is utilized, stored, and protected, as well as the choices and rights available to you under relevant privacy regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and Google Play Developer Policies.

💡 Core Principle: Cube Tank is designed as an offline single-player arcade game. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect your personal identity information such as your real name, email, phone number, address, or contacts list.

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

To ensure proper game functionality, stability, anti-cheat protection, and ad-supported monetization, we and our authorized third-party service providers (Google AdMob) may collect the following categories of data:

A. Device and Technical Identifiers (Automated)

  • Device Information: Device brand and model, operating system version, hardware specifications, system language, and network state (e.g., Wi-Fi or cellular network).
  • Anonymous Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID). This identifier is used exclusively by Google AdMob for ad frequency capping, fraud detection, and delivering relevant ads in accordance with Google's policies.

B. Local Gameplay Data (Stored on Your Device)

  • Game Progress & Stats: Campaign stage progression (Stages 1–50), endless mode wave records, unlocked weapon types (AP Shell, Shotgun, Laser, Flamethrower, Freeze Shell, Homing Missile, Plasma Cannon), tank workshop upgrade levels (HP, Armor, Engine, Damage, Reload, Shield, Bullet Speed, Fortune), collected in-game coins, and audio/vibration settings.
  • Storage Method: All gameplay progress is stored locally on your device via standard Android local storage (SharedPreferences). It is never transmitted to any private unauthorized server.

C. Crash and Diagnostics Data

  • Anonymous Crash Telemetry: In the event of an application error or crash, non-personally identifiable diagnostic stack traces may be gathered to diagnose system incompatibilities, fix bugs, and optimize performance.
🛡️ Explicit No-Collection Guarantee: We do NOT collect your real name, government IDs, phone number, physical address, financial card details, microphone recordings, camera feeds, or precise GPS location data.

3. Android System Permissions

To deliver a smooth arcade gaming experience, Cube Tank requests only standard, non-sensitive Android permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET Internet Access: Required for loading Google AdMob advertising content (app open ads, interstitial stage transitions, and optional rewarded revive ads).
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Network State: Enables the application to detect network availability to optimize ad caching and prevent unnecessary network requests while offline.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE Haptic Vibration: Powers tactile vibration feedback during cannon firing, recoil, and explosion impacts. You can freely turn haptic vibration ON or OFF at any time inside the in-game Settings menu.

4. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Our game incorporates certified third-party SDKs provided by Google. These third parties process technical data in compliance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob Google AdMob SDK (Google Play Services Ads):
    • Purpose: Displaying non-intrusive banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads.
    • Data Processed: Google Advertising ID (GAID), IP address (for coarse geographic location), device model, ad interaction logs, and diagnostic data.
    • Privacy Policy: Google Privacy & Terms
    • Ad Technology Details: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

5. Data Security, Storage & Retention

We implement industry-standard technical measures to safeguard your information.

  • Local Storage: Your game save data resides exclusively on your local device. If you clear the app data or uninstall the game, all locally stored progress and coins will be permanently removed.
  • Transmission Security: All network requests made by third-party ad providers are encrypted using modern HTTPS (TLS/SSL) cryptographic protocols.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Global Standards)

Cube Tank is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 where applicable by law, such as in certain EU member states).

If we discover that personal data has been inadvertently collected from a child without verified parental consent, we will promptly take steps to delete such data from our records. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at thjhsoft@gmail.com.

7. Your Privacy Rights & Choices (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your geographic jurisdiction, you hold specific rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access & Deletion: You have the right to request access to or deletion of data collected about you.
  • Advertising ID Reset / Opt-Out: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising Identifier or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your Android device settings:
    Go to: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID / Delete advertising ID.
  • In-Game Controls: You can adjust sound, music, recoil physics, and screen shake directly within the game's System Settings menu.

8. Changes and Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in game features, legal regulations, or third-party service requirements. Any revisions will be published directly on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
