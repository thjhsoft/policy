Privacy Policy for LyricDrop for Suno

Last Updated: September 16, 2026

Overview

LyricDrop for Suno is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how our Chrome extension handles information and data when you use it.

Data Collection and Usage

LyricDrop for Suno does NOT collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, account credentials, browsing history, or user activity. All operations take place entirely within your local browser.

  • No External Servers: The extension does not operate any remote backend servers or analytics trackers.
  • No Account Information: The extension does not ask for or store your Suno username, password, or authentication credentials.
  • Local Processing: Lyrics, song titles, and synchronized timestamps are fetched directly from your active Suno tab in memory and converted locally into LRC or SRT files on your device.

Permissions Disclosures

LyricDrop requests minimal permissions strictly necessary for its single purpose:

  • activeTab: Used temporarily only when you explicitly click the LyricDrop action icon to read the currently active Suno song tab.
  • scripting: Used to execute the client-side extraction script that reads lyrics and synchronized timestamps from the active tab.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or transfer any user data to third parties. We do not use user data for creditworthiness, lending, or any advertising purposes.

LyricDrop for Suno 隐私政策（中文版）

数据处理原则

LyricDrop 仅在你主动点击扩展图标后，在本地读取当前已打开并登录的 Suno 歌曲页面。所有歌词解析、时间轴提取与 LRC/SRT 文件导出均在你的浏览器本地进行，不会上传至任何第三方服务器。

不收集、不存储、不上报

本插件不运营任何收集个人信息的后端服务器，绝不收集、存储、跟踪或出售任何个人信息、账号密码、歌曲数据、下载记录或浏览历史。

权限使用说明

  • activeTab：仅在用户主动点击插件图标时临时获取当前页面的访问权限。
  • scripting：用于在当前 Suno 标签页中执行本地提取脚本，读取歌词与同步时间轴。
