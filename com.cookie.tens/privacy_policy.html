Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Tens! ("we", "our", or "us"). We value and respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile puzzle game.

2. Information Collection & Usage

We do not require you to create an account or provide personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number to play the game.

  • Game Progress & Preferences: Your high scores, stars, unlocked wallpaper themes, sound settings, and game statistics are stored locally on your device.
  • Device & Diagnostics: Standard technical information (e.g., device model, OS version) may be processed anonymously by system frameworks to ensure stability and smooth performance.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Services

To support ongoing development and keep the game free to play, we integrate trusted third-party advertising services (such as Google AdMob):

  • Third-party ad networks may use anonymous advertising identifiers to serve relevant ads according to applicable platform guidelines.
  • In our Kids Mode (Math Enlightenment), personalized ads are strictly disabled, and ad requests comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) standards.
Google AdMob Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. Data Security

Your local game data is kept securely on your device. We implement industry-standard security practices to protect any transmitted operational data from unauthorized access or alteration.

5. Children's Privacy

We care deeply about children's safety and privacy. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under 13. When Kids Mode is active, all COPPA-compliant flags are strictly enforced.
