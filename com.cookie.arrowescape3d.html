Privacy Policy

Tap Block 3D: Arrow Away
Effective Date: October 26, 2026

1. Overview

Welcome to Tap Block 3D: Arrow Away ("the Game"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of all players. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, and disclosure when you enjoy our 3D spatial logic puzzle game.

Our core philosophy is simple: we provide a relaxing, premium puzzle experience without harvesting or selling personal user identities.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect any Personally Identifiable Information (PII). Specifically, we do not request or store:

  • Your real name, physical address, or postal code
  • Your email address or phone number
  • Your social security or government identification number
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone access
  • Your biometric or precise GPS location data

3. Local Storage on Your Device

All your puzzle progress is stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This includes:

  • Completed level indexes and 3-star ratings
  • Accumulated puzzle coins
  • Unlocked block skins and scenic backgrounds
  • Audio, vibration, and language preferences

This data remains strictly on your device and can be erased at any time using the in-game Reset Progress button or by clearing the app data in Android system settings.

4. Advertising & Google AdMob

To keep the Game free for players worldwide, we integrate third-party advertising services provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC).

AdMob may automatically collect certain diagnostic and device-level information through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID)
  • Device make, model, operating system version, and system language
  • Ad interaction events (impressions, clicks, completed views) for frequency capping and fraud prevention
  • Coarse network-derived location (country/city level, non-precise)

Google uses this information to deliver compliant ads and measure campaign performance in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob Policies.

Google Play Families Policy Compliant

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Tap Block 3D: Arrow Away is an all-ages spatial logic game designed to be family-friendly and relaxing. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction).

Where applicable, ad requests are configured in compliance with COPPA (tagForChildDirectedTreatment) and Google Play's Families Policy, ensuring no behavioral retargeting or interest-based profiling of children.

6. User Choices & Managing Ad Preferences

You have full control over advertising identifiers and personalized ads on your Android device:

  • Reset Advertising ID: Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID.
  • Delete Advertising ID: Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID to opt out of interest-based advertising across all apps.
  • Reset Game Progress: Tap the Settings icon in the Game menu and select Reset Progress.

7. GDPR & CCPA / CPRA Rights

Under regulations such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA):

  • You have the right to access, rectify, or erase any personal information held about you. Because we do not operate user accounts or cloud databases containing your identity, no identifiable records exist on our servers.
  • We do not "sell" or "share" personal user data with third-party data brokers.

8. Security

We value your trust in playing our game. We implement industry-standard practices and rely on Google Play Services' hardened infrastructure to ensure that any network communication for ads is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS protocols.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be reflected with an updated "Effective Date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
