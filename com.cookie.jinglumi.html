Privacy Policy

App Name: Merry Candy – Christmas Match

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

1. Local Game Data

Merry Candy stores your level progress, earned stars, coins, booster inventory, achievements, collections, and preferences (audio, graphics, and piece size) strictly locally on your device. We do not operate personal user accounts or store your personal game progress on external servers.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

In ad-enabled builds, Merry Candy uses Google AdMob to display App Open and Interstitial advertisements. Google AdMob and its mediation partners may collect and process device identifiers, IP address, diagnostic logs, and ad interaction data in order to serve and measure ads.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. European Economic Area (EEA), UK & Switzerland Consent (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, Merry Candy requests your consent via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) before initializing ad measurement and loading personalized or non-personalized ads. You can review or withdraw your consent at any time from the Privacy Options (GDPR) button inside Settings.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the applicable age of digital consent.
